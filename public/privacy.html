Privacy Policy

This application is an internal tool of the Scheduling Seminar (schedulingseminar.com). It is used only by the seminar organizers to update the title, description and playlist of the seminar's own YouTube videos after each talk.

The application accesses only the seminar's own YouTube channel. It does not collect, store, share or sell personal data of any other users. Access can be revoked at any time in the Google account security settings.
